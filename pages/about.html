GET TO KNOW ME

Hey, I'm Mohammed Rayyan

I'm a Graphic Designer, Engineering Student & ROM Developer based in Tamil Nadu, India. I founded Ninety5 Studio in 2021 with the mission of blending design excellence with technical precision.

My journey spans across multiple creative and technical disciplines — from crafting pixel-perfect brand identities and UI systems to building custom Android ROMs and full-stack web applications. I believe great design and solid engineering are two sides of the same coin.

When I'm not pushing pixels or compiling kernels, you'll find me exploring the latest in open-source tech, experimenting with 3D renders in Blender, or editing videos in DaVinci Resolve. I'm driven by curiosity and a relentless desire to build things that matter.

VALUES WHAT DRIVES ME

MY PRINCIPLES

Clarity Over Cleverness

I believe in clean, readable solutions. Whether it's code or design, simplicity wins. The best work communicates instantly without needing explanation.

Accessibility First

Great design is inclusive design. I build with semantic HTML, proper contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen reader support as non-negotiables.

Performance Matters

Every millisecond counts. I optimize aggressively — lazy loading, minimal dependencies, critical CSS inlining — to deliver fast, delightful experiences.

Open Source Spirit

I give back to the community that helped me grow. From custom ROMs to web tools, sharing knowledge and code openly is how we all level up.

Design with Purpose

Every visual choice is intentional. Color, spacing, typography — each decision serves the content and the user, never just aesthetics alone.

Never Stop Learning

Technology evolves daily. I stay current by experimenting, building side projects, and diving deep into new tools, frameworks, and techniques.
