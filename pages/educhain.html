EduChain ID

Your Verified Academic Identity

EduChain ID is a verified digital identity and credential platform for students, institutions, and recruiters. It enables educational institutions to issue cryptographically signed academic credentials, allowing students to build a portable, verifiable academic identity that can be trusted anywhere.

Instead of relying on self-reported achievements or unverifiable certificates, EduChain ID uses modern cryptographic standards such as RSA digital signatures, W3C Verifiable Credentials, and Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) to ensure that every credential can be independently verified.

Core Features

Verified Credentials

Institutions issue digitally signed certificates with tamper-proof cryptographic verification. Public verification endpoints and QR code validation ensure instant authenticity checks.

Portable Identity

Students maintain a unified digital academic identity. Credentials are exportable in W3C Verifiable Credential formats with shareable profiles and credential links.

Institution Dashboard

Secure credential management dashboard with key rotation, signing infrastructure, batch issuance, and full credential lifecycle tracking.

Recruiter Discovery

Search for verified student profiles. View credential authenticity instantly. Shortlist and evaluate candidates based on verified achievements.

Offline Verification

Credentials can be verified without relying on EduChain servers. Self-contained verification bundles for third-party validation.

Collaboration & Networking

Student collaboration requests, project groups, peer endorsements, activity feeds, and rich academic portfolios.

Who It's For

Students

Build a verified academic identity and showcase achievements with trusted credentials. Maintain a portable profile trusted across institutions and employers.

Educational Institutions

Digitally issue and manage academic records securely and efficiently. Maintain full control over credential issuance and verification.

Recruiters

Discover and hire talent based on verified credentials instead of self-reported claims. Faster, more trustworthy hiring without manual background checks.
